01 — PERSONAL & DIGITAL SAFETY

Your safety is
not negotiable.

Small preparations can make a difference. Use these starter steps to think ahead, protect your information, and identify people you trust.

  • ✓
    Create a safety planChoose trusted contacts and agree on a check-in plan.
  • ✓
    Protect your digital lifeUse unique passwords, review location sharing, and secure your accounts.
  • ✓
    Know where to seek helpIdentify local support services and emergency contacts before you need them.
If you are in immediate danger

Move to a safer place if possible and contact local emergency services or a trusted person. In India, call 112 for emergency assistance. Use a safe device if you think yours is monitored.
